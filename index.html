"Greater Dandenong is one of Australia's most multicultural places. That's not a footnote — that's the story."

The Dandenong Local is an independent, free newsletter written by a local, for locals. More than 150 languages are spoken across Greater Dandenong. The LGA has the highest proportion of overseas-born residents of any in Victoria. That complexity deserves coverage that's honest, respectful, and genuinely curious — not tokenising, not sensational.

— Topher, Editor
